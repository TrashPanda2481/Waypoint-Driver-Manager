-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
No other third-party code
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Waypoint's own components (CLI, GUI, core, engine, sources, platform) take no
external NuGet dependencies. They build only against the .NET base class
libraries and documented Windows APIs.

OEM driver catalogs (Dell, Lenovo, HP) and any driver packages are fetched
from the respective vendors at runtime, only when you opt in. That catalog
data and those packages are the property of their vendors and are not
redistributed with Waypoint.
